Tell Me Why

Channel automation

A private, owner-operated tool that prepares illustrated educational videos and publishes content approved by the Tell Me Why channel owner.

What the application does

The application creates scripts, narration, illustrations, captions, thumbnails, and publishing metadata from ideas supplied by the channel owner. Completed packages are sent to the owner for review before publishing to connected YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok accounts.

Who can use it

This is a personal-use application operated by the Tell Me Why channel owner. It is not offered as a public service, and unrelated users cannot connect their social-media accounts.

Account data

The application requests only the permissions needed to identify connected channel accounts and publish owner-approved media. It does not sell account data or use that data for advertising.
