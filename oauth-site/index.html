Tell Me Why

Channel automation

A private, owner-operated tool that prepares illustrated educational videos and publishes approved videos to the Tell Me Why YouTube channel.

What the application does

The application creates scripts, narration, illustrations, captions, thumbnails, and upload metadata from ideas supplied by the channel owner. Every completed package is emailed to the owner for review. It uses Google OAuth only to upload approved videos and thumbnails to the owner's YouTube channel and to schedule their publication.

Who can use it

This is a personal-use application operated by the owner of the Tell Me Why channel. It is not offered as a public service, and it does not allow unrelated users to connect their YouTube accounts.

Google user data

The application requests YouTube permissions so it can upload and manage videos on the connected channel. It does not sell Google user data or use that data for advertising.
