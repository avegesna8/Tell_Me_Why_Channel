Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Tell Me Why Channel Automation accesses, uses, stores, and protects Google user data.

Application operator

This personal-use application is operated by Abhinn Vegesna for the Tell Me Why YouTube channel. Privacy questions can be sent to abhinn.varma@gmail.com.

Google data the application accesses

After the channel owner grants consent, the application receives OAuth authorization to upload videos and thumbnails and to manage the status and scheduled publication of those uploads. The application may receive YouTube video IDs, upload status, scheduled publication times, and resumable-upload session information associated with videos it submits.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to upload owner-approved videos and thumbnails to the connected Tell Me Why YouTube channel, keep those uploads private during processing, schedule publication at times selected by the owner, and report the resulting YouTube links and status to the owner.

Storage and protection

OAuth client credentials and the refresh token are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets. Workflow state, generated media, YouTube video IDs, and resumable-upload state are stored in a private Google Cloud Storage bucket. Review messages are sent to the owner's email account. Access is limited to the application operator and the service identities needed to run the workflow.

Retention

Generated project media is scheduled for deletion from the private production bucket seven days after successful YouTube scheduling. Small workflow records, including video IDs and completion status, may be retained to prevent duplicate uploads and support operational history. OAuth credentials are retained until the owner revokes access or replaces them.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with data brokers. Data is disclosed only to service providers needed to operate the workflow, such as Google Cloud, GitHub Actions, Gmail for owner notifications, and YouTube for the requested upload and scheduling actions, or when required by law. Script, image, and voice generation providers do not receive the channel's Google OAuth token.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke the application's Google access from the Google Account connections page. The owner may request deletion of retained application records by emailing the address above. Revocation stops future API access but does not automatically delete videos already uploaded to YouTube.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
